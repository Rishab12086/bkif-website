AI-Laxmi

Her Voice, Her Savings, Her Future—Empowering Dreams through AiLaxmi

AiLaxmi is a voice-first AI companion on WhatsApp that helps its users to build savings habits, protect their money from inflation, and eventually participate in India’s capital markets — without ever touching their money.

AiLaxmi

Innovation for Impact

AiLaxmi is at the heart of the BankersKlub India Foundation’s programmatic interventions, as our proprietary, free, voice-first AI companion. It serves as the cornerstone of our ‘innovation for impact’ strategy. More than just a tool, AiLaxmi is a paradigm shift in how India approaches its financial inclusion goals. By enabling high-fidelity financial management through simple voice conversations on WhatsApp, we can effectively dismantle the triple barriers of literacy, technology, and trust that have long kept underprivileged and underserved women and semi-skilled youth on the fringes of the formal economy.

Our unique "No-Custody" model is our core USP: AiLaxmi guides beneficiaries to build robust savings habits and navigate money market instruments without ever taking possession of their funds. By ensuring that every rupee stays securely in the user’s own account while fostering the confidence to invest, AiLaxmi transforms passive account holders into active capital market participants. In doing so, BKIF doesn’t just fulfill a mandate; we revolutionize the government’s vision of Atmanirbhar Bharat by giving every Indian the digital voice to command their own financial destiny and growth.

In the Field

AiLaxmi in Our Pilot Villages

The Problem AiLaxmi is Solving

Millions of women in rural India are financially invisible.

Many earn small, irregular incomes. They keep cash at home — vulnerable to theft, emergencies, and inflation. They have Jan Dhan bank accounts they don't use. Few have ever been taught how to save, budget or invest.

And after chit fund and Ponzi scams have wiped out the savings of millions of depositors across India, many trust no one with their money.

No app, bank, or government scheme has solved this because the problem isn't access — it's trust and habit.

Why Now

Three Things Have Converged to Make AiLaxmi Possible

01

AiLaxmi Speaks Your Language

AiLaxmi can already speak around 22 Indian languages, with many more on the way. Open-source Indian language AI models now make natural voice conversations possible at near-zero cost. This was impossible even 2 years ago.

02

SEBI Opened the Door

Micro SIPs at small monthly amounts, with simplified KYC, mean a woman with a small income can legally participate in India’s equity markets for the first time.

03

WhatsApp is Already There

No app download is needed. The platform is already on their phones and already trusted.

The BKIF Promise

AiLaxmi is built by its committed and passionate Founder, Manideep Bora. He lives by one non-negotiable principle: we will never betray the trust of women who have already been betrayed in the past.

  • We will never touch their money
  • We will never promise returns
  • We will never hide our finances
  • We will grow slowly and honestly
  • We will shut down before we compromise

The women of India don't need another scheme. They need a friend they can trust. That's what AiLaxmi is.
