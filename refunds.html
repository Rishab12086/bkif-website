Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for review by BKIF's legal adviser or CA before publishing. This note disappears when preview mode is turned off in config.js.

Donations to BankersKlub India Foundation are put to work quickly, so they are generally not refundable. We will refund a donation in these cases:

  • You were charged twice, or charged the wrong amount, because of a technical error.
  • You made a donation by mistake and tell us within 7 days.
  • The donation came from someone we cannot legally accept it from, such as a foreign national. We return such donations to the original payment method.

How to Ask for a Refund

Email info@bkifoundation.org with your name, the amount, the date and your Razorpay payment ID. We reply within 48 hours. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days.

Tax Certificates

If a donation is refunded, we cannot include it in our annual statement to the Income Tax Department, and no tax certificate will be issued for it.
